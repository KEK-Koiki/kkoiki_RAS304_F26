ELECTRICAL REVIEW / BENCH PLAN
1. Before connecting the Nano or USB, power from a current-limited supply. Confirm center-positive jack wiring, fuse continuity, VIN_FUSED, +5 V and absence of shorts. Start with a low current limit appropriate to the disconnected board.
2. Confirm physical regulator pin orientation: L7805 IN=1, GND=2, OUT=3. Heatsink design remains required: at 12 V and 250 mA, nominal dissipation is 1.75 W, excluding regulator quiescent loss.
3. Nano VTG edge 51 receives +5 V; VOFF 55 is grounded; VBUS 56 is NC. This follows Microchip DS40002186B section 3.3.2 for external power. Verify the actual board/socket orientation before connection. Nano symbol is a passive connector representation; ERC does not enforce firmware pin directions.
4. TCST2103 bare sensors use four physical leads. The drawn pin numbers are logical IDs, not an approved footprint. Check Vishay D+/E+ markings and wire LED cathode and transistor emitter to cable common ground. J2-J5 pin 1 LED drive, 2 collector signal, 3 ground.
5. With approximately 1.25 V LED drop, each 220 ohm resistor gives about 17 mA and dissipates about 64 mW at 5 V. Four emitters draw about 68 mA before Nano/logic loads. Measure actual load before final fuse and heatsink selection.
6. Probe TP4-TP7 blocked and unblocked: verify raw low/high levels, U2 amplified levels and clean U3 logic edges. Nominal non-inverting gain is 1.5. A high raw input drives the amplifier into positive saturation; verify recovery and rail levels on real hardware. If pulse shape/timing is poor, revisit gain/bias/filter with instructor rather than assuming ERC proves analog suitability.
7. The 4.7k/1nF network has a nominal 33.9 kHz pole and 4.7 us time constant. Phototransistor impedance and wiring affect the response. Choose encoder slots and maximum shaft RPM before accepting it: per-channel pulse frequency = slots * shaft RPM / 60. Account for fourfold quadrature edge counting and gearing.
8. Mount each A/B pair 90 electrical degrees apart. Rotate both directions and verify the phase order at RB0-RB3. Establish a home reference because these are incremental encoders.
9. Configure RB0-RB3 digital inputs and the chosen UART PPS on RC6/RC7. DEBUG_LED is RA3 (edge16), DEBUG_BTN is RA4 (edge17); UART RX is RC7 (edge19). RA0/RA1 spare are edges43/44. Check netlist and symbol labels before firmware.
10. Test SW1 bounce. R19=10k and C11=1uF give nominal 10 ms recharge, but do not guarantee debounce for every switch. Verify Schmitt input configuration and add stable-state firmware debounce if needed.
11. Fit JP1 for encoder TX operation and remove for isolation. Agree J6 pin1 TX/pin2 RX, 5 V logic levels, ground8, physical 2x4 numbering, upstream/downstream chain and power-off behavior with the team before connection.
