    Assembly extras and fit checks¶

    A1 needs two 1×28 female sockets plus the existing Nano. F1 needs two compatible clips and one cartridge fuse. JP1 needs a shunt. Add two encoder harnesses, the keyed 8-conductor team cable, mounting standoffs and any required regulator heatsink. J1 is selected as CUI PJ-102AH; the supplied adapter photo does not confirm voltage, polarity or 2.1 mm inner size. Verify the real class parts against the 1:1 print before release. C8/C9/C10 are bottom-side 0805 capacitors. LEDs use custom pin 1 anode / pin 2 cathode footprints.
